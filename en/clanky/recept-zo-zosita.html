Zuzka's spinach from a notebook: how to get a handwritten recipe onto your phone

18 November 2026 · Ján Šikuta

When Zuzka moved out of her mum's home, she took a notebook of recipes she had written in her own hand. One of them is spinach: five lines, no grams, everything by eye.

The recipe as Zuzka wrote it

Ingredients: oil, plain flour (about 1 teaspoon), frozen spinach, milk, 3-4 cloves of garlic, salt and ground black pepper.

  1. Roux: oil, plain flour, about 1 teaspoon.
  2. Take off the heat and add a little milk.
  3. Put the frozen spinach into the roux and cook until it is mushy.
  4. Add more milk if needed.
  5. Add 3-4 cloves of garlic crushed with salt, and ground black pepper.

From notebook to phone in a few minutes

In Coolinarka, tap the plus and choose Take or choose a screenshot. Take a photo of the recipe or pick it from Photos. The app reads the text right on your phone and splits it into ingredients and steps. The photo of the notebook stays with the recipe as its cover photo until you replace it with a photo of the finished dish.

The app is not perfect. Nor is our handwriting.

Handwriting is not print. The paper is creased, the ink fades and everyone writes differently. In our video the app read the Slovak word špenát (spinach) as “Spenát”, without its accent, and turned kašovitý (mushy) into “kasorty”. If Coolinarka cannot make sense of your handwriting (even a pharmacist would struggle with a doctor's handwriting), just tap Edit and correct it by hand. It is a few words, not retyping the whole recipe.

Zuzka did not write down grams. If you want to see calories in Coolinarka Plus, add amounts to the ingredients, otherwise the app has nothing to calculate from.

Why bother

A notebook can survive a lot, but not everything. It gets mislaid with every move, sometimes stays behind at mum's, and lying open next to the stove it happily collects a few green spinach stains. A recipe in Coolinarka is on your phone, tablet and computer, with a shopping list and step-by-step cooking. Keep the photo of the notebook with Zuzka's handwriting as the cover, clean and stain-free, or replace it with a photo of the finished dish. Everyone can then choose their own side dish.
