Cooking without scrolling: ingredients under each step and a timer on the Lock Screen

24 November 2026 · Ján Šikuta

Floury hands, something spitting on the stove, and meanwhile your phone has gone dark. You unlock it with your elbow and look for how much flour it was supposed to be. Cook mode was made for exactly this moment.

One step, large text, the display stays on

In cook mode you always see just one step, in large text, and the display stays on while you cook. A tap takes you to the next step. The Aa button gives you four text sizes, and if you prefer the whole picture, you can show all steps at once.

Ingredients right under the step

Below the text of each step are the ingredients you use in it, with amounts for the number of servings you chose. If you double the recipe, you see double amounts here too. You do not have to go back to the ingredient list to check how much of what.

A timer from the text

If a step mentions a time, for example “bake for 20 minutes”, the app offers a timer for it. You start it with one tap and it keeps running even when you close the app. On iPhone you see it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, on Android in a notification. Several timers can run at once, even from different recipes.

Since version 3.1, the timer rings like an alarm when it finishes, even when your phone is locked (on iPhone with iOS 26, even when you are wearing a watch). You choose the sound in More: a microwave ding or beeps, a system sound, or no sound. On Android, allow notifications and exact alarms when you start your first timer, otherwise it may ring late.

Reading aloud and units

You can have a step read aloud when you do not want to look at the display. Recipes from American websites with cups and degrees Fahrenheit can be switched to grams and degrees Celsius.

No account, no internet

Cook mode works offline and without signing in. It is free, just like saving recipes, the shopping list and the meal plan.

Using Android? Help us test

On iPhone and iPad, Coolinarka is on the App Store. The Android version is still in testing, and Google will release it to everyone only when enough people really use it. Anyone with a Google account can join: three short steps. It helps most if you cook with it, keep it for at least 14 days and leave us a few words right on the Coolinarka page in Google Play.
