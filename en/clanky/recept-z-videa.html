Recipe from a video: when the method is only spoken

10 November 2026 · Ján Šikuta

Fifteen seconds, music, quick cuts and a voice saying: two spoons of butter, onion until translucent and a handful of parsley at the end. The caption is just a heart. Recipes like this are tasty, but writing them down is a chore.

What the app does with a video

On iPhone, iPad and Android, open a video from your gallery (on iPhone, from Photos) or share it to Coolinarka, or record the video in Instagram with screen recording. The app transcribes the spoken word and reads the text that appears in the video. From both, it puts together the ingredients and the method. Transcription and text reading happen on your phone, and the video is not sent anywhere.

Sped-up voices, which are common in short videos, are slowed down by the app and transcribed again. From a screen recording, it removes things that do not belong in the recipe, such as the song title or the author's name along the bottom edge.

Completing a recipe you already have

If you saved a recipe from a caption and the method is missing, you can complete it from the video. The app combines what you already have with what was said in the video.

What to watch out for

A transcript is only as good as the sound. With loud music or fast talking, check the amounts, especially for baking. If the author never says the amounts, the app will not make them up. Speech transcription needs a language your phone supports, and for some languages the system downloads it the first time you use it.

Why on your phone

Video recipes often come from your private recordings. We did not want to send them to someone else's server. Transcription on the device is slower than in the cloud, but your video stays with you.
