Coolinarka

Recipes from the web and Instagram in one place, ready for cooking.

  • Save with one tap. In Instagram, Facebook or Safari tap Share and choose Coolinarka.
  • Organized. Meal types, tags, favorites and your own collections.
  • Shopping list. Ingredients from several recipes add up, you tick them off in the store, and you can send them to Reminders or a shared family list.
  • Fast search. By title, ingredients or method, regardless of accents.
  • Cooking mode. Large text, one step per screen, timers, the screen stays on.
  • Recipe from video. Turn a video or a screen recording into a recipe; speech is transcribed right on your iPhone. Facebook works with sign-in inside the app.
  • Your cover photo. Crop, rotate and filters.
  • iPhone and iPad. Recipes sync through iCloud between devices with the same Apple account, and you can see the status in More.
  • Import and export. Back up your collection or move it to another app.
  • Your look. Nine color themes with their own app icon, dark mode and larger text.

Coolinarka Plus

Recipes, import, export, backups, iCloud and cooking mode are free. A one-time Plus purchase (no subscription, can be shared with your family) adds calories and nutrients: a per-serving estimate from the ingredients or the value from the recipe source, an ingredient breakdown with your own corrections, a calorie filter and sorting, and nutrition in the PDF. Values are approximate and not medical advice. The calculation runs on your device. It also adds a PDF cookbook: your recipes as a beautiful book with a cover, contents and chapters, to print or give as a gift.
