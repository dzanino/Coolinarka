Coolinarka

Recipes from the web and Instagram in one place, ready for cooking.

  • Save with one tap. In Instagram, Facebook or Safari tap Share and choose Coolinarka.
  • Organized. Meal types, tags, favorites and your own collections.
  • Fast search. By title, ingredients or method, regardless of accents.
  • Cooking mode. Large text, one step per screen, timers, the screen stays on.
  • Import and export. Back up your collection or move it to another app.

Coolinarka Plus

Recipes, import, export, backups, iCloud and cooking mode are free. A one-time Plus purchase (no subscription, can be shared with your family) adds calories and nutrients: a per-serving estimate from the ingredients or the value from the recipe source, an ingredient breakdown with your own corrections, a calorie filter and sorting, and nutrition in the PDF. Values are approximate and not medical advice. The calculation runs on your device.
