Privacy Policy

Coolinarka does not collect any data about you.

What the app does with data

  • Your recipes, photos and notes are stored in the app on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they sync through your private iCloud account. On Android, the system backup may save them to your Google account. The developer has no access to them.
  • When you save a link, the app downloads the recipe page and its preview image directly from your device. Links are not sent anywhere else.
  • Text recognition from screenshots happens on your device. On Android the app uses the Google ML Kit library for this: the image and the recognized text never leave your phone, but ML Kit sends Google technical data for diagnostics and usage statistics (device manufacturer, model and OS version, app version, a per-installation identifier that does not identify you or your device, recognition speed and error codes). The data is encrypted in transit and Google does not pass it to third parties. Details: Google ML Kit data disclosure.
  • The shopping list is written to Reminders only when you tap the button and never leaves your device.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it needs no account.

Plus purchase

The optional one-time Coolinarka Plus purchase is processed by Apple (App Store). The app only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid and collects no payment or personal data. The calorie calculation runs on your device and your recipes are not sent anywhere for it.

Children

The app is not designed to collect data from children and collects no data at all.
