Support

We are happy to help with Coolinarka.

How do I save a recipe from Instagram or Facebook?

Open the post, tap Share and choose Coolinarka. If the recipe text cannot be read from the post, the app saves the link and image and you can complete the recipe by pasting the caption or a screenshot.

Why was my Instagram recipe saved incomplete?

Instagram and Facebook do not allow reading content without signing in. Copy the post caption and paste it into the recipe, the app splits it into ingredients and steps.

How do I back up my recipes?

In More choose Export. This creates a .coolinarka file that you can save to Files or send with AirDrop. Import works the same way.

Does my collection sync between my devices?

Yes, through your private iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on.

How do I delete my data?

Delete recipes in the app. Removing the app deletes the data from the device, and data in iCloud can be removed in Settings > your account > iCloud > Manage Storage.
